Documentation — movie consumption visualization

For the D3 visualization, my dataset provided the following fields: title, rating, and year of release.

I used it to show how movies are distributed across release year and rating. I chose a bubble chart: each film is a bubble, colored by the decade it was released in. You can sort bubbles either by the decade they appeared in or by the rating you gave them.

This technique felt like a good fit because the bubbles quickly convey how many films there are, and the animated grouping makes patterns by rating and release year easy to see.
