Description

  • Each bubble is one film. Bubble size reflects rating. Bubble color matches the era in which the film was released (see legend above).
  • Group by decade: arranges bubbles by release decade.
  • Group by rating: arranges bubbles by rating; 0–0.5 shares one band, then each half-point step, up to 10.
  • Group at center: pulls all bubbles into a single cluster in the middle of the chart.
  • Hover: a tooltip shows title, year of release, and rating.
